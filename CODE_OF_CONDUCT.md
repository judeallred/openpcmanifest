# Code of Conduct

OpenPCManifest adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other channel where people represent the project.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus
on what is best for the people using and building the project. Harassment, personal attacks and
publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through the
email address on [Jude Allred's GitHub profile](https://github.com/judeallred). Reports are handled
promptly and in confidence. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they judge inappropriate, following the
enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
