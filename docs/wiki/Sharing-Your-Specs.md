## Copy a short summary

Click **Copy summary** and choose:

- **Markdown**: Reddit, GitHub, Discord.
- **BBCode**: most hardware and gaming forums.
- **Text**: email or anywhere else.

## Save a full report

**File → Save report as** writes:

- **HTML**: a single page that opens in any browser, good for attaching to a support request.
- **Markdown**, **BBCode** or **Text**: the full report in that format.
- **JSON**: for scripts and tools.

## Hiding private details

Values that identify your PC or you are marked private: serial numbers, MAC and IP addresses, Windows product ID, Wi-Fi network names, computer and user names, printer ports and volume serials. They're replaced with `[redacted]` when hiding is on.

- **Copies and exports are redacted by default.** Change this under **View → Hide private details in exports and copies**.
- **On screen**, tick **Hide private details** in the toolbar, for example before sharing a screenshot.

Raw snapshots (see [[Snapshots and Compare]]) are **never** redacted, so don't post those publicly.
